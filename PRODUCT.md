# Resume

## Register
brand

## Users
HR and technical hiring leads screening a Chinese resume for a senior full-stack engineer at a small or medium internet company.

## Product Purpose
Present verified experience clearly in a two-page PDF, with editable Markdown and working project links.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, capable. The user requested Claude-inspired visual restraint and careful typography.

## Anti-references
Uniform heavy body text, dense undifferentiated paragraphs, weak employer/project distinction, and decorative dashboard cards.

## Design Principles
- Lead with recent experience and individual responsibility.
- Distinguish employer, role, dates, project, and contribution.
- Preserve accurate project stages and working hyperlinks.
- Use typography and alignment to make the document easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Selectable Chinese text, legible print sizes, high contrast, and visible link labels.

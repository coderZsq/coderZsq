# Resume visual system

## Direction
Claude-inspired warmth, expressed through a restrained terracotta accent and clear typesetting. This is a recruiting document intended for reading and printing.

## Palette
- Paper: white (#FFFFFF).
- Ink: near black (#272622).
- Secondary text: #66635D.
- Accent and links: #98543F.
- Rules: #E3E0DA.

## Typography
Heiti SC Light for Chinese body text; Heiti SC Medium for headings and emphasis. Helvetica and Helvetica Bold for Latin runs. Body 11pt with 19pt leading. Name 28pt. No all-bold body and no artificial bold mapping.

## Layout
A4, two pages, 44pt horizontal margins. Name and target role lead the first page. Contacts are secondary. Company/role align left and employment dates align right. Project labels are subordinate to employers. Sections use restrained rules and varied vertical spacing. Second page has a compact running header and larger spacing between employers to balance the two page endings.

## Verification
Render and inspect both pages; confirm two-page length, selectable text, no clipping or overlaps, and eight working hyperlink annotations.
